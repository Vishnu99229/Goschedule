HOW IT WORKS

Three Steps. Zero Friction.

1

Scan

Customer scans the QR code at the table. Menu loads instantly. No app, no signup.

2

AI Recommends

Our upsell engine (powered by GPT-4o-mini) analyzes order patterns and suggests the right add-ons at the right moment.

3

Order & Track

Order goes live to the kitchen. Café owner sees orders, behavior, and revenue impact in the admin dashboard.
